SHEET 1. JULY 6, 1986.
Camp set on the dry rise west of the pad. Tents up by 1600. Latrine dug. Radio check good.

SHEET 2. JULY 8, 1986.
Baseline run the length of the stones. Engineer unhappy with how far they stand off it. No pattern he can see.

SHEET 4. JULY 9, 1986.
Cores at the base of 1 through 10. All stop at the same depth. Tube not damaged. Will try further along.

SHEET 6. JULY 10, 1986.
Cores 11 through 41. Same depth. Told the engineer the tube is marked wrong. It is not marked wrong.

SHEET 8. JULY 12, 1986.

SHEET 10. JULY 13, 1986.

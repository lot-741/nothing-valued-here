All seven signs of stone 31, line one, agree with Variant Three, in order. This can be explained. People think alike and the problem suggests the form.

Sign 7 is backwards. Like ours.

Ours is backwards because of the typist. It was caught in '85 and corrected in pencil. There is only the one copy.

M. in the typing pool keeps her own index in twenty-six drawers. Anything with its dates the wrong way round goes in from the back.

The lichen on stone 31 grows across sign 7.
